Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

1. Introduction

Jira Dashboard ("we", "our", or "the app") is a web application that allows users to view and manage their Atlassian Jira issues through a convenient dashboard interface. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data when you use our service.

2. Data We Access

When you connect your Jira account, we request the following permissions:

  • read:jira-work – To read your Jira issues, projects, and boards.
  • read:jira-user – To read your basic Jira user profile (display name, avatar).

We only request read-only access. We never modify, create, or delete any data in your Jira account.

3. Data Storage

We do not store your data on any server.

All Jira data is fetched directly from Atlassian's API and displayed in your browser. Your OAuth access token is stored only in your browser's local storage and is never sent to or stored on our servers. The only server-side operation we perform is a secure token exchange during the login process, which does not retain any data.

4. Third-Party Services

This app interacts with Atlassian's APIs to fetch your Jira data. We recommend reviewing Atlassian's own privacy policy for details on how they handle your data.

The app is hosted on Vercel.

5. Cookies

This app does not use cookies. Authentication state is managed through browser local storage, which you can clear at any time by logging out or clearing your browser data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to any third parties. Your Jira data is only used to display your dashboard within the app.

7. Security

We use industry-standard security practices including OAuth 2.0 with PKCE (Proof Key for Code Exchange) for authentication, and all API communications are encrypted via HTTPS. Your Atlassian client secret is stored securely on the server side and is never exposed to the browser.

8. Your Rights

You can at any time:

  • Log out to remove your access token from local storage.
  • Revoke access by visiting your Atlassian Connected Apps settings.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository.
